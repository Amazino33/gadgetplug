# How to Use the Till

A simple guide for shop attendants. This is for the **computer/desktop till**, not a phone.

There are 4 parts to your day:

1. Open your shift
2. Sell
3. Submit cash
4. End the day

---

## 1. Open Your Shift

Do this first, before you sell anything.

1. Open the till.
2. You will see a green button. Tap **Start your shift**.
3. The screen will ask: **"How much is in your drawer?"**
4. Count the cash that is already in the drawer. This is the money you met on ground — not money you have sold yet.
5. Type that amount into the **Opening float** box.
6. Tap the green button **Open the till**.

You can now start selling.

**Important:** This is your starting money, not your sales. Do not include anything you have not sold yet.

**If you see "A day is still open":**
This means a past day was not closed properly. You must finish it first. Tap the button showing that old date (it will say something like **End of day 12 Sep**) and follow the steps to close it. Then start your shift again.

---

## 2. How to Sell

### Ring up a sale

1. Search for the product, or scan its barcode.
2. It will appear in your cart.
3. If you need to change how many, tap **Change Qty**.
4. If the customer is buying on credit, tap **Customer** first and pick or add them.
5. When the cart is ready, tap the big green button **PAYMENT**.

### Choose how the customer is paying

You will see 4 ways to pay:

| Button | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | Customer is paying with cash |
| **POS / Card** | Customer is paying with an ATM card on your POS machine |
| **Bank Transfer** | Customer is transferring money |
| **Debt (Owing)** | Customer is not paying now — this needs a customer name already picked |

**For Cash:**
1. Tap **Cash**.
2. Type how much the customer gave you in **Amount Tendered**. You can also tap **Exact**, or a quick amount like **₦5,000**.
3. The screen shows **Change Due** if you need to give change.
4. Tap **Complete** (or press **Enter**) to finish.

**For POS / Card:**
1. Tap **POS / Card**.
2. Charge the amount shown on your POS machine.
3. Tap **Complete** to finish.

**For Bank Transfer:**
1. Tap **Bank Transfer**.
2. Type the last 4 or more digits from the transfer alert, in **Transfer Reference**.
3. Tap **Complete** to finish.

**For Debt (Owing):**
1. Make sure you already picked the customer (tap **Customer** before payment if you have not).
2. Tap **Debt (Owing)**.
3. Tap **Complete** to finish.

### If a customer is paying two ways (Split Payment)

Example: Customer pays part cash, part transfer.

1. On the payment screen, tap **Split Payment** at the top.
2. Type how much is Cash, how much is POS, how much is Transfer — for however many ways they are paying.
3. Tap **Complete** to finish.

### Other useful buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Discount** | Reduce the price before payment |
| **New Sale** | Start a fresh cart |
| **Suspend Sale** | Save this cart for later without losing it (customer will come back) |
| **Void Order** | Cancel this sale completely |
| **Return** | Take back an item a customer already bought |

### Logging money you spend from the till (Expense)

If you take money out of the till to pay for something small — like transport for a delivery — you must record it.

1. Tap **Menu** at the top.
2. Tap **Expense**.
3. Choose a reason: **Transport / delivery**, or **Something else**.
4. Type the amount.
5. You can write a short note in **"What was it for?"** — this is optional.
6. Tap **Record it**.

---

## 3. How to Submit Cash

Submitting cash means handing your money to your manager or whoever collects cash, and having them confirm they received it.

**You do not have to submit every shift.** If you have not submitted, that is fine — it will simply say you have not submitted cash yet. We will explain what happens to it in Part 4.

### Step A — Record the handover (in the till)

1. Tap **Menu**.
2. Tap **Submit Cash**.
3. The screen shows **"You are holding ₦X"** — this is the cash you have taken, minus anything you already handed over.
4. Tap **"Handing it to…"** and choose the person collecting the cash.
5. Type the amount you are giving them.
6. If the amount does not match what the till expects, you must type a short reason why.
7. Tap **Record handover**.

You will now see: **"{Reference} recorded. ₦X handed to {name}. Waiting for them to confirm they got it."**

**This needs internet.** If your till shows no connection, it will tell you to wait — do not force it, or it may show you as short for money you have not actually lost.

### Step B — Show the code (in the office, to your manager)

The person confirming your money does this on the **panel**, not the till.

1. Ask your manager to open **Money → Cash Submissions** on their screen.
2. They tap **Show code** next to your handover.
3. A QR code appears on their screen. **They** show it to **you**.
4. You (or they) scan it.
5. On the scanning screen, the collector taps **Confirm cash handover** if the amount is correct, or **Record a dispute** if it is not.

### What the status means

After you submit, you will see one of these next to your handover:

- **pending** — Recorded, but not yet confirmed. Still waiting.
- **confirmed** — They have received it and agreed the amount. Done.
- **disputed** — They say the amount does not match. This needs to be sorted out with your manager.

## 4. How to End the Day

Do this when your shift is finishing.

1. Tap **Menu**.
2. Tap **End of Day**.
3. The screen asks: **"How much cash have you counted?"** Count your drawer and type it in. Tap **Next**.
4. The screen asks: **"What is the total on your Moniepoint machine?"** Type the total shown on your POS/card machine. Tap **See how you did**.
5. You will see **"Where you stand"** — what you counted, next to what the till says you should have.
6. Tap **Continue**.
7. If there is a difference, write a short note explaining it in **"Your note"**.
8. Tap **Finish cash-up**.

If your numbers match, the button will say **Finish cash-up**. If they do not match and you have written a note, it will say **Send this and finish**. If you truly cannot explain a difference, there is a button that says **"I cannot explain it — finish anyway"** — only use this if you really do not know why the numbers are different.

**Important — you do NOT need to submit your cash before ending the day.**

If you have not submitted your cash (Part 3), you can still close your day. The cash you are holding stays recorded as still with you — it is not lost, and it is not forgotten. You (or your manager) can settle it later.

Your manager sees your final numbers after you finish.

---

## Quick Summary

| Part | What you do | Main button |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Open shift | Count drawer, type it in | **Start your shift** → **Open the till** |
| 2. Sell | Scan/search, add, choose payment | **PAYMENT** → **Complete** |
| 2. Log spending | Record money you paid out | **Menu → Expense → Record it** |
| 3. Submit cash | Hand cash over, get it confirmed | **Menu → Submit Cash → Record handover** |
| 4. End the day | Count drawer, compare, finish | **Menu → End of Day → Finish cash-up** |

**Remember:**
- Opening cash is what you *met*, not what you *sold*.
- You do not have to submit cash every day.
- You do not need to submit cash before you can end the day.
